Anime-World

This web application is created to bring anime fans something new and unique to build a community. In this application you can get updates on popular animes, get inspiring quotes everyday, and help new fans to expand their catalog.

This application can help you find new anime or old anime shows you haven't seen before by going through Anime Match. It takes your preferred genre and Episode length to find you a match.

Lastly we want to create a community so we have a community Post where you can comment your opinion on anime,theories and more.
